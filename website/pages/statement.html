The formal theorems

The independent statement uses finite-dimensional right modules, represented in Lean as modules over Aᵐᵒᵖ. A complete finite family of indecomposable modules expresses finite representation type. The radical of A is Mathlib’s Jacobson radical.

For Theorem B, the radical J of the category of indecomposable modules gives the spaces of irreducible maps J/J2; their dimensions are the arrows of ΓA. The translate τX is defined by an almost split sequence 0 → τX → E → X → 0. The mesh category is the linear path category of ΓA modulo the mesh relations, the sums of all paths τX → Y → X. Standardness asks for a k-linear equivalence of this mesh category with the indecomposable modules, sending each vertex to its module. The statement also records that every space of irreducible maps has dimension at most one, so these are exactly the mesh relations of ΓA.

MultiplicativeBasisDeformations.Statement.theoremA and Statement.theoremB prove these statements. The bridge identifies the translate defined by almost split sequences with the one of the library and normalizes the mesh coefficients of the library to one.
