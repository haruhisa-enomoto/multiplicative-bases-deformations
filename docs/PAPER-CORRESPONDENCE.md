This table maps every numbered statement of *Multiplicative bases and
deformations of representation-finite algebras* to the Lean declarations that
formalize it. It refers to the revision of October 17, 2026 (r41), 105 pages,
whose TeX, PDF and hashes are in [`manuscript/`](manuscript/README.md).

Lean is `v4.33.1`, with Mathlib `0df444a360eaa60ab8c11dca51a86af692955474` as
the only external Lake dependency. Declaration names are relative to the
namespace `MultiplicativeBasisDeformations`; files are relative to
`MultiplicativeBasisDeformations/` unless they start with `Statements/`,
`Foundations/` or `Refinements/` (also in that directory).

Conventions. Modules are right modules, formalized as modules over the opposite
algebra. Composition is left to right, as in the paper, which agrees with
`f ≫ g` in Lean. The field and the algebra live in one universe.

Coverage words: *proved* means a Lean theorem without `sorry` states the
result; *formalized definition* means the notion is defined in Lean; the
examples are not formalized. A proved row may state the result in an
equivalent or more general form, or prove it by a shorter route than the
paper. The docstrings of the listed declarations describe these forms.
